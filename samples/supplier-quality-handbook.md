# Supplier Quality and Inbound Logistics Handbook

## 1. Supplier qualification

Every vendor must be approved by Procurement before the first purchase order is issued. Approval
requires a valid certificate of insurance and a signed quality agreement. A certificate of
insurance states the insurer, the coverage amount and the expiry date; Procurement rejects
certificates that expire within 30 days.

## 2. Receiving

Inbound shipments arrive at the receiving dock of a warehouse. The haulier (our contracted
carrier) remains responsible for the goods until the receiving clerk signs the delivery note.

On arrival, every pallet is split into inventory lots. An inventory lot is a quantity of a single
product received together; each lot is identified by a lot number and records its expiry date.
Inventory lots are stored in a warehouse location until they are consumed or shipped.

## 3. Quality inspection

A quality inspection is performed on each inventory lot before it is released to stock. The
inspector records the inspection result (accepted, rejected or on hold) and the inspection date.

When an inspection fails, the inspector raises a nonconformance report against the supplier that
provided the lot. A nonconformance report describes the defect, its severity and the corrective
action requested from the supplier.
